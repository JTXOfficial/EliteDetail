Our Paint Correction Process

A methodical approach to restore your vehicle's paint to perfection

01

Paint Inspection

We begin with a thorough inspection using specialized lighting to identify all defects and assess the condition of your paint.

02

Paint Thickness Measurement

Using a paint depth gauge, we measure the thickness of your paint to determine how much correction can be safely performed.

03

Surface Preparation

The vehicle is thoroughly washed and decontaminated using clay bar treatment to remove embedded contaminants.

04

Paint Correction

Using professional-grade polishers and compounds, we carefully remove defects through a multi-stage process tailored to your vehicle.

05

Refinement

Fine polishing to enhance gloss and clarity, removing any holograms or marring from the correction stage.

06

Surface Inspection

Multiple light sources are used to verify the correction results and ensure all defects have been properly addressed.

07

Protection Application

A high-quality wax, sealant, or ceramic coating is applied to protect the newly corrected paint and enhance its appearance.

08

Final Inspection

A comprehensive final inspection to ensure every panel meets our high standards before returning your vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our paint correction service

How long does paint correction take?

The time required for paint correction depends on the size of the vehicle, the condition of the paint, and the level of correction needed. A single-stage enhancement typically takes 1-2 days, while a multi-stage correction can take 2-4 days. We prioritize quality over speed to ensure the best possible results.

Will paint correction remove all scratches from my car?

Paint correction can remove most surface-level defects, including swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, and oxidation. However, deep scratches that penetrate through the clear coat into the base coat or primer cannot be fully removed through polishing. During our initial assessment, we'll provide an honest evaluation of what results you can expect.

How long will the results of paint correction last?

The longevity of paint correction results depends largely on how the vehicle is maintained afterward. With proper care, including using proper washing techniques and avoiding automatic car washes, the results can last for years. We recommend applying a protective coating after correction to extend the life of your perfect finish.

Is paint correction safe for my vehicle?

When performed by our experienced professionals, paint correction is completely safe. We use paint thickness gauges to measure your clear coat and ensure we're working within safe limits. Our technicians are trained to use the least aggressive method necessary to achieve the desired results, preserving as much of your factory clear coat as possible.

What's the difference between paint correction and buffing?

Buffing is a general term often used to describe a quick polish that temporarily enhances gloss but doesn't address underlying defects. Paint correction is a systematic, multi-step process that actually removes defects from the clear coat through controlled abrasion. While buffing might make a car look better temporarily, paint correction delivers lasting results by actually fixing the problems.
