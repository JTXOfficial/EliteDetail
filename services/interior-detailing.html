Our Interior Detailing Process

A methodical approach to restore your vehicle's interior

01

Initial Assessment

We begin by assessing the condition of your interior to identify problem areas and determine the appropriate cleaning methods.

02

Thorough Vacuuming

Using professional-grade equipment, we vacuum all carpets, seats, trunk, and hard-to-reach areas to remove loose dirt and debris.

03

Steam Cleaning

We use steam cleaning to sanitize and deep clean carpets, upholstery, and other fabric surfaces, eliminating bacteria and odors.

04

Stain Removal

Specialized products and techniques are applied to remove stubborn stains from carpets, seats, and other interior surfaces.

05

Dashboard & Console

All plastic, vinyl, and hard surfaces are thoroughly cleaned, treated, and protected with appropriate products.

06

Leather Treatment

Leather surfaces are cleaned, conditioned, and protected to prevent cracking and maintain their supple feel.

07

Glass & Mirrors

All interior glass surfaces are cleaned to a streak-free shine using professional-grade glass cleaners.

08

Final Touches

We apply finishing touches including air freshening, trim dressing, and a final inspection to ensure everything is perfect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our interior detailing service

How long does interior detailing take?

A comprehensive interior detailing typically takes 3-5 hours depending on the size of your vehicle and its condition. Vehicles with significant stains or odor issues may take longer. We focus on quality results rather than rushing through the process.

Can you remove all stains from my upholstery?

While we can remove most common stains, some stubborn or set-in stains may be permanent. Factors such as the type of stain, how long it's been there, and the material of your upholstery all play a role. We'll always be honest about what results you can expect during our initial assessment.

How often should I get my car's interior detailed?

For most drivers, we recommend interior detailing every 4-6 months. However, this can vary based on how you use your vehicle. If you have children, pets, or frequently eat in your car, you might benefit from more frequent detailing. Regular maintenance cleanings between full details can also help maintain a clean interior.

Will interior detailing eliminate all odors in my car?

Our interior detailing service can eliminate most common odors by addressing them at the source rather than just masking them. However, severe odors (such as smoke damage or pet accidents that have penetrated deep into materials) may require specialized odor elimination treatments, which we can provide as an additional service.

Are your cleaning products safe for my car and passengers?

Yes, we use professional-grade products that are effective yet safe for your vehicle's materials and for you and your passengers. Many of our products are eco-friendly and non-toxic. If you have specific concerns or sensitivities, please let us know, and we can adjust our product selection accordingly.
