Exterior Detailing

Our comprehensive exterior detailing service is designed to restore your vehicle's exterior to showroom condition. We use premium products and professional techniques to clean, correct, and protect your car's paint, wheels, and trim.

Our Exterior Detailing Process

We follow a meticulous step-by-step process to ensure the best results

01

Pre-Wash Rinse

We begin with a thorough pre-wash rinse to remove loose dirt and debris, preventing scratches during the washing process.

02

Wheel & Tire Cleaning

Special cleaners are used to remove brake dust, road grime, and dirt from wheels and tires, restoring their appearance.

03

Two-Bucket Hand Wash

We use the two-bucket method with premium pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash mitts to safely clean your vehicle's exterior.

04

Clay Bar Treatment

A clay bar is used to remove embedded contaminants from the paint surface that washing alone cannot remove.

05

Paint Polishing

We carefully polish the paint to remove minor scratches, swirl marks, and oxidation, restoring gloss and clarity.

06

Wax or Sealant Application

A high-quality wax or synthetic sealant is applied to protect the paint and enhance its shine for months to come.

07

Trim & Glass Treatment

All exterior trim, rubber, and glass surfaces are cleaned and treated with appropriate products for a complete finish.

08

Final Inspection

We perform a thorough inspection to ensure every detail meets our high standards before returning your vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our exterior detailing service

How long does an exterior detailing service take?

A complete exterior detailing typically takes 3-5 hours depending on the size of your vehicle and its condition. For vehicles with significant paint correction needs, it may take longer. We prioritize quality over speed to ensure the best results.

How often should I get my car's exterior detailed?

For optimal protection and appearance, we recommend exterior detailing every 3-4 months. However, this can vary based on your driving conditions, where you park your vehicle, and your local climate. Vehicles frequently exposed to harsh elements may benefit from more regular detailing.

What's the difference between a car wash and exterior detailing?

A car wash simply removes surface dirt and provides basic cleaning. Exterior detailing is a comprehensive process that includes deep cleaning, contaminant removal, paint correction, and protection. Detailing addresses issues below the surface and provides lasting results that a regular car wash cannot achieve.

Will exterior detailing remove all scratches from my car?

Standard exterior detailing can remove light scratches, swirl marks, and minor imperfections. However, deep scratches that penetrate through the clear coat may require more intensive paint correction services or professional repainting. During our initial assessment, we'll let you know what results you can expect.

How long will the results of exterior detailing last?

The results of exterior detailing typically last 2-3 months, depending on the products used and how you maintain your vehicle. Premium waxes and sealants can provide protection for up to 6 months. For longer-lasting protection, consider our ceramic coating services, which can last 1-5 years.
